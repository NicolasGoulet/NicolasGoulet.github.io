How to read this map. The fills are schematic snapshots of political control, not exact modern-style borders and not a map of religion or ethnicity. “Reconquista” is a conventional and debated label for many intermittent wars, alliances, truces, raids, and settlements—not one continuous, unified campaign.

Chronology and broad territorial stages follow the Metropolitan Museum of Art, its account of the Almoravid and Almohad periods, and a Cambridge University Press overview of the intermittent Christian conquests. Coastlines and present-day borders are included only for orientation.
