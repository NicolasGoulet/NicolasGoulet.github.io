<header class="editorial-intro">
  <p class="eyebrow">Language · cognition · computation</p>
  <p class="editorial-intro__lead">
    I study how learners—human and artificial—move from linguistic experience
    toward structured, meaningful representations.
  </p>
</header>

<div class="research-grid">
  <section>
    <span>01</span>
    <h2>Language acquisition</h2>
    <p>
      What can children’s learning tell us about the structure of language,
      and what can computational models reveal about the learning problem?
    </p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <span>02</span>
    <h2>Grounding &amp; meaning</h2>
    <p>
      My earlier work on Minimal Grounding Sets led to an enduring interest in
      how symbols connect to one another, to experience, and ultimately to meaning.
    </p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <span>03</span>
    <h2>Humans &amp; machines</h2>
    <p>
      I am interested in comparisons that take both sides seriously: models as
      computational objects and human learners as more than benchmarks.
    </p>
  </section>
</div>
