## Intro

Why would one spend countless hours reading boring letters exchanged between even more boringer *petits bourgeois* from some lost place in Switzerland ? Don't these people eat cats and dogs ?

Nevertheless, *Julie, Ou la Nouvelle Héloïse*, is one of the most important novel of my life. It is also one of the most important novel of the European literature, considered a masterpiece  by the likes of Kant, Goethe, Schiller, Balzac, Tolstoi amongst others. Schopenhauer himself deemed it one of the fours novel that must be read in one's life (this list includes *Wilhelm Meister*, which we will discuss in a future blogpost).

*Toute relecture dépourvue d'une prise de notes est une perte de temps* - any second reading of a book without thorough note-taking is a waste of time, if I may paraphrase the author of *La Nouvelle Héloïse*. This novel ended up becoming a therapeutic exercise for Rousseau, and I hope it will become apparent as this blog series progresses. 

Writing about this novel is harder than ever. The experts, whom will have often read even Rousseau's letters, will deem this exerice futile and full of mistakes, errors and misconceptions. For the general reader, aside from my possibly contagious enthusiasm, how are they to be hooked on this endless exchange of letters?
